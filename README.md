# STal

STal is a technology and software solutions company founded by Shrushti Talati.

## About Us
The idea behind STal was born from a simple vision — to solve real-world technical problems and make technology more accessible, useful, and impactful for everyone.

We believe technology should not only serve businesses, but also everyday people — regardless of age, skill level, or digital experience.

## Our Mission
Our goal is to build systems that are:
- Simple  
- Powerful  
- Scalable  
- User-friendly for all types of users  

## What We Do
STal focuses on turning ideas into practical digital solutions, including:
- Software Development  
- Website Development  
- Application Development  
- Technical Problem Solving  
- Digital Solutions for Individuals and Businesses  

## Our Vision
To create technology that bridges the gap between complexity and usability, making digital tools accessible for everyone.
